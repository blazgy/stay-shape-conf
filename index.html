Conference overview

A working meeting about staying power

Stay & Shape brings together 70 participants from Austria, Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Moldova, Montenegro, Serbia and other EU countries to examine youth migration and the digital reshaping of labour.

Political context

Migration pressure and digital change are colliding

Across Southeastern Europe, young people are leaving in growing numbers, driven by low wages and unstable employment. Meanwhile, AI, automation and digital management are reshaping the quality of work for those who stay.

Why this matters now

The conference links traditional organising questions with the future of work agenda, so that digital transformation strengthens workers rather than deepening precarity.
